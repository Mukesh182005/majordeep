# Data statement

Every dataset and third-party model the paper plan uses, with its source,
licence and split policy. Licences marked "checked 2026-10-14" were read from
the source page on that date; anything else is **to be checked** before the
data is used in the paper. Raw data lives outside the repository
(`DEEPSENTINEL_DATA`); only manifests and per-item scores are committed.

## 3. Local data that must not be used as evidence for the paper

| Folder | What it is | Why it cannot support a paper claim |
|---|---|---|
| `data/raw/massive_audio` (167,772 clips: 83,886 "bonafide", 83,886 "spoofed") | Synthesised with NumPy by `scripts/generate_massive_audio_dataset.py`: low-frequency modulation as "speech", high-frequency artefacts and jitter as "TTS". | Not speech. `checkpoints/audio_detector.pt` was trained on it: its summary reports a validation set of 12,582 + 12,582 = 25,164 windows, which equals the `val` count of `data/processed/audio_massive`, and AUC 1.0 / EER 0.0 on that set. |
| `data/processed/audio`, `data/processed/audio_massive` | 4-second windows of the above. The summary reports a "speaker-disjoint" split, but there are 167,772 "speakers" (one per clip). | Same as above; the split is not by real speaker. |
| `data/raw/massive_social`, `data/raw/expansion_300k` | Filtered, re-encoded copies of faces-140k **training** images. Their "diffusion" and "inpaint" classes are PIL blur and patch filters on StyleGAN faces, not diffusion output (per the generator script docstrings). | Derived copies of training data; not AI-generated in the sense the paper uses. |
| `accuracy_test/` (120 images in the repository) | Copied from `massive_social` by `backend/scripts/build_real_dataset.py` (file names `instagram_real_*`, `diffusion_fake_*`, `snapchat_real_*`). | Copies of faces-140k training images, so not independent of training. |
| `evaluation/V2-DIAGNOSTIC-01` (95 images) | Diagnostic gallery from earlier phases. | Provenance not documented. Must be removed from T2 by perceptual hash if any of it is reused. |
| `backend/storage/uploads`, `storage/uploads` | Files uploaded to the running application. | Unlabelled user data; never used for training or evaluation. |
